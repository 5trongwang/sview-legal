One Wide Photo, One Seamless Swipe

Give your photo more room by spreading it across two slides in SView. The details appear larger, and the scene unfolds in one seamless swipe.

Steps

  1. Open Views, set Slices to 2, and choose a ratio you like. Try 4:5 for a portrait carousel on Instagram.
  2. Use Add → Photo or drag a photo from the Photo Tray onto the canvas. Select the photo, then pinch and drag until it fills both slides.
  3. In Preview & Export, choose Carousel and swipe to check the fit. Tap Camera Roll to save. Upload both images in order as one carousel post.
