Turn a Wide Photo into a Slow Pan

With Scroll in SView, a wide photo becomes a video that pans from one side to the other. Each detail gets its turn on screen.

Steps

  1. Open Views and set Slices to 2. Try 9:16 for a vertical video.
  2. Drag your photo onto the canvas. With the photo selected, tap Position, then Width×2 to span both slices. Pinch to zoom and drag to frame your subject.
  3. Open Preview & Export and choose Scroll. For a slower pan, increase Duration. Tap Camera Roll to save the video, then share it on social media.
