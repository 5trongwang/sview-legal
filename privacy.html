Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026  ·  Applies to: SView (iOS)

SView ("we", "our", or "the App") is a photography tool that helps you display wide-format photos on social platforms. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Photos

SView requests access to your photo library solely to import photos for editing and to save exported results back to your library. Your photos are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

1.2 Anonymous Identifier

After initial setup and any iOS Photos prompt have finished, SView starts its online services and uses Firebase Authentication to sign in anonymously. When the sign-in succeeds, Firebase creates a randomly generated user identifier (UID). This identifier:

  • Is not linked to your name, email address, or any personal information
  • Cannot be used to identify you outside the App
  • Is used to authenticate and de-duplicate optional What's New likes
  • May be converted into a pseudonymous StoreKit app account token when you start a purchase; it does not decide whether you have Pro access

1.3 Subscription & Purchase Data

All subscription purchases, billing, renewals, refunds, and Family Sharing eligibility are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. SView determines Pro access on your device from Apple-verified StoreKit entitlements and does not store a separate subscription entitlement record in Firebase Firestore.

If you have turned on Usage Data, SView may record limited purchase-flow events:

  • The selected product ID and plan (weekly, monthly, or yearly)
  • Whether the StoreKit flow was verified, unverified, pending, cancelled, or failed
  • The StoreKit environment (for example, production or sandbox)

SView's custom purchase-flow events do not include your transaction ID, original transaction ID, app account token, price, currency, payment details, or revenue. Firebase Analytics may separately generate standard StoreKit lifecycle events and associated product, currency, or value fields according to SDK and platform behavior. These events are controlled by the same Usage Data choice. App Store Connect remains our source of record for commercial subscription metrics.

1.4 App Preferences

Language and appearance preferences are stored locally on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device.

1.5 Usage & Analytics Data

If you turn on Usage Data during initial setup, or later enable Share Usage & Diagnostics in More, SView uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand which features are useful and where workflows fail. Analytics collection is disabled by default and the App works normally if you leave it off. You can change this choice at any time in More.

  • In-app interaction events — for example, editor feature use, import/export outcomes, paywall sources, and which font or frame is included in a completed export
  • A pseudonymous app-instance identifier and basic technical and session information, including device model, operating system, app version, app lifecycle and engagement statistics, and an approximate region derived from your IP address
  • Limited SDK delivery and performance metadata generated by Firebase analytics dependencies

Usage Data never includes your photos, project content, filenames, or EXIF metadata.

This analytics data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. We do not use the device advertising identifier (IDFA).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email address, or contact information
  • Precise location data
  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • Your photos, project content, filenames, or EXIF metadata (all photo processing happens on your device)

3. Third-Party Services

SView uses the following third-party services provided by Google (Firebase):

  • Firebase Authentication – for anonymous user identification
  • Firebase Firestore – for aggregate What's New like counts and anonymous-UID de-duplication records
  • Firebase Analytics – only after your choice to share pseudonymous usage analytics for aggregate reporting

These services operate under Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

All in-app purchases and subscription billing are handled by Apple. We do not receive or store any payment information.

4. Data Retention

Your anonymous UID and any associated What's New like de-duplication record are retained until deletion is requested or the service record is removed under our retention practices. Analytics data already transmitted before you turn sharing off may remain in aggregated Firebase/GA4 reporting according to the configured retention period; turning sharing off stops new collection and resets the analytics data stored locally by the SDK. Uninstalling and reinstalling generally creates a new app instance and may create a new anonymous UID. Your subscription remains with your Apple ID and can be restored through StoreKit; SView does not restore it from a Firebase subscription database.

5. Children's Privacy

SView is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
